PROJECT OVERVIEW
================

This project transforms a 1byone vinyl record player into a fully self-contained
Spotify-powered streaming turntable while preserving the physical ritual of vinyl.


ITERATION 1
-----------

Iteration 1 focuses on:

  - Removing all stock electronics
  - Installing custom Raspberry Pi-based electronics
  - Maintaining the original motor and spinning platter
  - Adding a 4-inch LCD display for album artwork
  - Delivering studio-quality RCA output
  - Running Spotify locally on the device
  - No analog blending yet
  - No secondary control interface yet
  - No stock circuit board

This phase establishes a clean digital backbone for future hybrid expansion.
